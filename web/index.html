The parts you'd otherwise pay a subscription for.

No feature is held back, metered, or reserved for a paid tier — there isn't one.

ERG mode control
Holds your trainer at each interval's target power over Bluetooth FTMS, so the resistance follows the workout instead of your legs.
Plans that adapt
Tell the coach your goal and your week, and it writes a periodized plan of any length. It then reshapes upcoming sessions around what you actually completed and how hard it felt.
Training load, done properly
Normalized Power, Intensity Factor and TSS computed the way coaches expect — plus weekly load, adherence and time-in-zone.
One calendar, four sports
Rides, runs, swims and lifts share a single calendar, so you can see a heavy Saturday coming before you schedule anything against it.
Workout builder
Build intervals against a live power profile, then save and reuse them anywhere in your plan.
Strava sync
Completed rides upload with full power data. Activities you record elsewhere come back in and count toward your plan.
